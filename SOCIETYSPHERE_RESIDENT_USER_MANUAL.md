# SOCIETYSPHERE — RESIDENT & APARTMENT OWNER USER MANUAL

**Target Audience**: Apartment Owners, Tenants, Residents, and Family Members  
**Language Support**: English & தமிழ் (Tamil)  
**Accessibility**: Easy Mode (Large Buttons, Senior-Friendly Interface) & Standard Mode  

---

## 1. LOGIN & ACCOUNT ACCESS
1. Open the SocietySphere web app at your society portal URL.
2. Enter your registered Mobile Number / Email Address and Password.
3. Select your Active Society from the society selector dropdown if you reside or own units in multiple societies.
4. If you prefer high-contrast large buttons, click the **Easy Mode** toggle at the top right corner.

---

## 2. RESIDENT DASHBOARD & INBOX
- **Unread Notifications**: Check the bell icon or open `/notifications` to view real-time announcements, emergency alerts, maintenance billing notices, and meeting invites.
- **Marking as Read**: Click **Mark Read** on any notification item to update your inbox state.
- **Easy Mode View**: Easy Mode displays simplified notifications with large high-contrast cards and one-click read toggles.

---

## 3. RESIDENT PROFILE & FAMILY / DELEGATES
- View unit occupancy details, ownership shares, and registered family members.
- **Adding Delegates**: Assign proxy delegates for voting or unit maintenance approvals with time-bound validity.

---

## 4. VEHICLE REGISTRATION & PARKING STICKERS
- Open `/vehicles` to register new two-wheelers, four-wheelers, or bicycles.
- Enter vehicle registration number, make/model, and upload RC/insurance documents.
- Upon society security verification, your vehicle RFID tag / parking sticker status will update to `VERIFIED`.

---

## 5. VISITOR MANAGEMENT & GUEST PRE-APPROVAL
- Generate instant digital visitor passes for expected guests, delivery agents, or cab drivers.
- Guest receives an SMS/WhatsApp pass code.
- Security gatekeepers verify the pass code at the gate terminal for fast-track entry.

---

## 6. COMPLAINTS & HELPDESK
- Open the Complaints section to raise service requests (e.g. plumbing, electrical, elevator, common area cleaning).
- Attach photos or evidence. Track real-time SLA progress and staff assignment updates until closure.

---

## 7. MAINTENANCE BILLS & ONLINE PAYMENTS
- Open `/billing` to view your monthly maintenance invoices, itemized charge breakdowns, and due dates.
- Click **Pay Now** to record online or offline payment details and instantly download immutable payment receipts.

---

## 8. FACILITY & COMMON AREA BOOKINGS
- Open `/facility-bookings` to reserve clubhouse halls, tennis courts, swimming pool slots, or guest rooms.
- View real-time slot availability, rules, and booking approval status.

---

## 9. COMMUNITY EVENTS & ANNOUNCEMENTS
- Open `/events` to explore society festivals, sports tournaments, and workshops.
- Register participants, view capacity limits, and download event passes.

---

## 10. EMERGENCY SOS BUTTON
- In case of fire, medical, security, gas leak, or elevator emergencies, click the prominent **🚨 EMERGENCY SOS** button on `/incidents`.
- Security guards and emergency responders will be dispatched immediately with critical alerts bypassing quiet hours.
