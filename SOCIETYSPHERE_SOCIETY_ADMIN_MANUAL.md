# SOCIETYSPHERE — SOCIETY ADMINISTRATOR & SECRETARY MANUAL

**Target Audience**: Society Administrators, Managing Committee Members, and Secretaries  

---

## 1. SOCIETY SETUP & PHYSICAL HIERARCHY
- Administer society configuration, block structures (Towers/Blocks), floor layouts, and unit numberings.
- Manage ownership transfers, co-ownership percentages, and move-in/move-out occupancy workflows.

---

## 2. MEMBER & RESIDENT DIRECTORY MANAGEMENT
- Approve or reject resident membership applications.
- Manage role assignments (`RESIDENT`, `TENANT`, `SECURITY`, `STAFF`, `VENDOR`).
- Conduct tenant background verification status checks and delegate revocations.

---

## 3. ANNOUNCEMENTS & MASS BROADCASTS
- Open `/announcements` to draft society broadcasts.
- Target announcements by block, floor, owner type, or society-wide.
- Schedule future broadcasts and track read receipt analytics.

---

## 4. GOVERNANCE, MEETINGS & RESOLUTIONS
- Open `/governance` to schedule Annual General Meetings (AGM) or Extraordinary General Meetings (EGM).
- Publish meeting agendas, track quorum completion, record formal resolution votes, and publish meeting minutes with action items.

---

## 5. EMERGENCY & INCIDENT TRIAGE
- Open `/incidents` to monitor active emergency alerts.
- Triage incident severity (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`), assign security or staff responders, and log containment actions.

---

## 6. MAINTENANCE WORK ORDERS & STAFF MANAGEMENT
- Open `/maintenance` and `/staff` to review helpdesk complaints, dispatch work orders to staff/vendors, monitor material consumption, and verify SLA compliance.
