# SOCIETYSPHERE — TREASURER & FINANCIAL ACCOUNTING GUIDE

**Target Audience**: Society Treasurers, Financial Auditors, and Accountants  

---

## 1. FINANCIAL CORE & GENERAL LEDGER
- Open `/finance` to manage the double-entry accounting ledger.
- **Chart of Accounts**: Manage assets, liabilities, equity, revenue, and expense accounts.
- **Journal Entries**: Post balanced debit and credit journal entries for operational income, vendor invoices, and banking transactions.

---

## 2. MAINTENANCE BILLING & INVOICE CYCLES
- Open `/billing` to configure monthly charge calculation rules (flat rate, area-based, head-count, or hybrid).
- Open billing periods and execute deterministic billing cycles.
- Review generated invoices prior to publishing to resident portals.

---

## 3. PAYMENTS & RECONCILIATION
- Record incoming resident maintenance payments (UPI, Cheque, NEFT/RTGS, Cash).
- Automatic FIFO payment allocation against unpaid invoices.
- Generate immutable sequential payment receipts (`REC-000001`).
- Perform bank statement reconciliation sessions and close financial periods.

---

## 4. FINANCIAL STATEMENTS & AUDIT
- Generate Income & Expenditure Statements, Balance Sheets, Trial Balances, and General Ledger reports.
- All financial entries are immutable; corrections require balanced journal reversals with full audit trails.
